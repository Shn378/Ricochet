What Ricochet is

Ricochet watches public data (news reports, satellite fire detections, internet outages, seismic sensors, aircraft, shipping and Wikipedia activity) and highlights places where it's running well above normal. It's an automated early-warning aid that points you to the original reporting. It is not a news service and doesn't verify events.

Please don't rely on it for safety

  • Nothing on Ricochet is confirmed unless a card says so, and then only because an official statement or two independent sources say so. Follow the links and read the original reporting.
  • Not for life-safety decisions. In an emergency, follow official warnings and the emergency services where you are. Ricochet can be late, wrong or offline.
  • Outages and fires have many causes. An internet outage or a fire near a site is shown as a signal with its cause not confirmed. We never label one an attack ourselves.
  • Data comes "as is" from the sources below, with their own gaps and errors. Some sources ask that their data isn't used for tactical decisions or local-scale conditions (NASA's LANCE disclaimer for fire data), and we pass that on.

Using what you see here

You're welcome to look, share links and quote cards with a link back. Please don't scrape the site or the API in bulk, resell it, or present it as verified fact. Each source keeps its own licence (below); some don't allow commercial use.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no adverts, no tracking cookies.
  • Your browser stores one setting on your device (whether you've seen the "What am I looking at?" box). It never leaves your device.
  • The site runs through Cloudflare, which processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver and protect it. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it sends them.
  • The server keeps standard short-term technical logs to keep the service running.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies and nothing that identifies you, only totals such as page views, device type, browser, country and the site that linked here.
