What am I looking at?

Ricochet watches public data for the early echoes of conflict and security incidents: news wires, fire-spotting satellites, internet outages, seismic sensors, aircraft, shipping and Wikipedia edits. Each is compared with what's normal for that place.

  • Rings on the globe are hotspots. Amber = Reported: two or more independent feeds above normal. Grey-blue = Signal: one feed well above normal.
  • Shaded countries have an ongoing or escalating situation in the news.
  • Lime marks natural hazards and weather warnings. They're not security incidents and are kept separate.
  • Cards explain why each hotspot is there and link to the original reporting. Swipe, use ‹ › or the arrow keys. Touching the tour pauses it.
  • Nothing here is confirmed. These are automated tripwires, not verified events. Follow the links to the source.
